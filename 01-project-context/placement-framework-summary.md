## Placement Framework: Planned vs Delivered

The full original brief is available at [`../06-supporting-evidence/Ikarians_-_MSc_Health_Data_Science_Placement_Framework.pdf`](../06-supporting-evidence/Ikarians_-_MSc_Health_Data_Science_Placement_Framework.pdf).

This section maps the planned placement framework against the work ultimately delivered and included in this repository. Its purpose is to provide transparency about how the scope evolved during the placement and to show how the contents of the repository relate to the original brief.
