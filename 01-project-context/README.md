# 01 — Project Context

## Ikarians

Ikarians is an early-stage digital health platform. Its product combines wearable data, primarily from Garmin devices, with a one-off onboarding questionnaire and a daily in-app lifestyle survey. These data feed an AI engine designed to generate patient-facing insights and clinician-facing dashboard alerts. The platform's underlying philosophy draws on Value-Based Healthcare and longevity research, including the Blue Zones literature, with an emphasis on extending healthy life expectancy rather than focusing solely on the treatment of disease.

## Placement Purpose

The placement brief states this directly:

"The purpose of this placement is not to build production systems. The aim is to help Ikarians transform complex, multi-source health data into meaningful, structured and actionable data for insights."

The original framework was structured across 10 weeks, covering familiarisation, data mapping, data quality assessment, AI-readiness design, insight discovery, product recommendations and a final roadmap. The full brief is retained at [`../06-supporting-evidence/Ikarians_-_MSc_Health_Data_Science_Placement_Framework.pdf`](../06-supporting-evidence/Ikarians_-_MSc_Health_Data_Science_Placement_Framework.pdf).

## My Role and Scope of the Placement

Working alongside the founding and technical team, my role was to develop an independent, evidence-based understanding of the platform's health data. This involved examining what data were collected, assessing their quality and reliability, identifying gaps and limitations, and considering how the data would need to be structured and improved to support future AI-driven applications.

The placement explicitly encouraged critical engagement with existing assumptions rather than simply documenting the platform as it existed. This shaped my approach throughout the work: I considered not only what data were available, but also whether they were sufficiently meaningful, reliable and interpretable to support the intended use cases.

To ground recommendations in established health and clinical evidence rather than general wellness assumptions, I have drawn a structured literature base covering areas including physical activity, sleep, mental health, nutrition, social relationships and longevity. This evidence base is summarised in [`evidence-landscape.md`](evidence-landscape.md), alongside a description of how Ikarians' internal clinical reference material informed the work.
